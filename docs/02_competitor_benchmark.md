# 02 - Head-to-Head Competitor Benchmark

**Target Practice:** Family Dental Care  
**Audited Local Competitor:** The Dental Studio Kayamkulam (Dr. Sajitha)  
**Audited Regional Leaders:** The Facets Dental, Scientific Dental Clinic  

---

## 1. Competitive Landscape Analysis

An exhaustive audit was conducted comparing the local #1 ranked competitor (**The Dental Studio Kayamkulam** led by Dr. Sajitha) alongside regional cosmetic leaders across Kerala (**The Facets Dental** and **Scientific Dental Clinic**). This analysis identified exact ranking vulnerabilities, content gaps, and high-impact strategic opportunities to establish market leadership.

## 3. Key Competitor Weaknesses Exploited

1. **Empty Blog Section on Competitor Sites:**
   - The Dental Studio has zero published blog articles, leaving high-volume patient search queries (such as *"invisible aligners cost Kerala"* and *"veneers cost Kayamkulam"*) completely uncontested.
2. **Unfinished Portfolio Pages:**
   - Competitors left placeholder developer text live on their public case galleries. Family Dental Care deploys clean, detailed case studies with exact timelines and clinical notes.
3. **Lack of FAQ Schema:**
   - While competitors list questions as plain text, Family Dental Care wraps all FAQs in structured JSON-LD data to claim Google SERP snippet real estate.
4. **Passive vs. Active Catchment Targeting:**
   - Instead of simply keyword-stuffing city names in the footer, Family Dental Care provides real distances, verified driving times, and direct Google Maps route links.
