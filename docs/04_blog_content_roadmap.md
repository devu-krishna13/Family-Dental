## 3. Detailed Article Outlines & Target Search Intent

### Article 1: Invisible Aligners Cost in Kerala (2026 Guide)
- **Target Keywords:** `invisible aligners cost Kerala`, `clear aligners price Kayamkulam`, `teeth straightening EMI Oachira`, `braces price Alappuzha`
- **Primary Search Intent:** Transactional / Commercial Investigation
- **Key Sections:**
  - Average cost breakdown in Kerala (₹45,000 to ₹1,50,000 depending on complexity).
  - Monthly EMI payment plans starting from ₹3,500/month.
  - Cost factors: Single arch vs dual arch, treatment duration (6 to 14 months).
  - Comparison with traditional metal braces costs.
  - Zero-obligation 3D digital smile scan at Family Dental Care.

### Article 2: Clear Aligners vs. Metal Braces Guide
- **Target Keywords:** `braces alternative Kayamkulam`, `clear aligners vs braces`, `best orthodontist near Oachira`, `invisible braces for adults`
- **Primary Search Intent:** Informational / Comparative
- **Key Sections:**
  - Social and professional challenges of metal braces for working adults.
  - Removability for meals, weddings, and oral hygiene.
  - Reduced clinic visits (every 6–8 weeks vs monthly wire tightening).
  - Clinical efficacy backed by NCBI orthodontic studies.
  - Commuting convenience for patients from Oachira and Kayamkulam.

### Article 3: Porcelain vs. Composite Veneers in Kerala
- **Target Keywords:** `dental veneers cost in Kerala`, `porcelain veneers Kayamkulam`, `composite bonding Oachira`, `smile makeover cost Kerala`
- **Primary Search Intent:** Commercial / Aesthetic Consideration
- **Key Sections:**
  - What are E-Max porcelain veneers vs direct composite bonding?
  - Longevity: 10–15+ years for porcelain vs 4–7 years for composite.
  - Stain resistance and enamel translucency differences.
  - Ideal candidates: severe tetracycline stains, chips, gaps, and worn edges.
  - Conservative enamel preparation without aggressive shaving.

### Article 4: Dental Implants vs. Bridges Cost & Procedure
- **Target Keywords:** `dental implants cost in Kerala`, `permanent tooth replacement Kayamkulam`, `tooth implant vs bridge`, `single tooth implant price`
- **Primary Search Intent:** Restorative / High-Value Investigation
- **Key Sections:**
  - The hidden cost of dental bridges: grinding down adjacent healthy teeth.
  - How titanium implants stimulate jawbone density and stop facial aging.
  - Step-by-step procedure: Computer-guided pain-free placement under local anesthesia.
  - Lifelong durability and overall investment value.
  - Solutions from single-tooth replacements to full-arch restorations.
